About Green Leaf Cafe

Founded in 2020, Green Leaf Cafe is committed to serving the freshest, most sustainable coffee and food in the city. We believe in supporting local farmers and using only organic, ethically sourced ingredients.

Our mission is to create a welcoming space where community and sustainability meet. Every cup of coffee and every meal is crafted with care for both our customers and the planet.
